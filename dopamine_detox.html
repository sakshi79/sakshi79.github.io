A detox that starts when your browsing slips.

Dopamine Detox is a Chrome extension that scores your browsing habits from 0 to 100. When your score drops too low, your feeds go on a ration for a few days. Use up your minutes and they close until midnight. You set the limits.

Your score comes from five habits

Install it and browse the way you normally do. After a couple of days your habits settle into a baseline, and the popup shows you which ones are pulling it down.

How a detox runs

  1. Your baseline settles

    Your score is the average of your last three active days, so one bad evening doesn’t set it off.

  2. When it drops too low, a detox starts

    For the number of days you choose, social sites get a daily allowance you set. Everything else stays open, and a notification tells you when five minutes are left.

  3. At the limit, social closes until midnight

    Instead of the feed, you get a short breathing exercise and a few things to do away from the screen. You can end a detox early whenever you want.

When time’s up, you get thirty seconds to breathe

This is what you see if you open a social site after your social time for the day is used up. Breathe in as the water rises and out as it falls. Three rounds take about thirty seconds. Try it here.

Then it suggests something else to do

  • Step outside for ten minutes without your phone.
  • Read a few pages of a paper book.
  • Write down the task you’ve been avoiding and do the first five minutes of it.
  • Stretch, make tea, or clear one surface.

A mirror, not a diagnosis

The score is estimated from how you browse. It doesn’t measure anything in your brain, and it isn’t medical advice. It’s there to show you a pattern you might not have noticed, and to give you a few days to change it.

Questions

Where does my browsing data go?

It stays in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and only site names and time totals are kept, never full web addresses or what’s on the page. Older days are deleted automatically.

Which sites count as social?

Sites like Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. The dashboard marks each social site with a dot, so you can always see what’s being counted.

Can I change the limits?

Yes. You choose how low your score has to drop before a detox starts, how much social time you get each day, and how many days a detox lasts. You can also edit which sites count as social.

Does it work on my phone?

Not yet, but an Android app is coming soon to the Play Store. For now, it’s a Chrome extension for desktop and laptop computers.
